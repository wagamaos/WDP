Practice 4 - Button vs Div

Understand why native buttons are more accessible than clickable div elements.

Quick review

A real <button> is keyboard-focusable, works with Enter and Space, has a built-in button role and supports the disabled state.

A clickable <div> does not provide these behaviours automatically.
