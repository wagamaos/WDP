Key Points

  • required: field cannot be empty.
  • minlength and maxlength: control text length.
  • min and max: control numeric limits.
  • step: controls acceptable number increments.
  • pattern: value must match a regular expression.
  • title: gives extra information about the expected format.
  • type="email": checks email format.
