Practice 2 - WCAG Levels

Learn the difference between WCAG A, AA and AAA.

Quick review

  • A: minimum requirements.
  • AA: standard target for most websites.
  • AAA: enhanced accessibility target.

Examples from the material: A includes alt text and keyboard access; AA includes 4.5:1 contrast for normal text; AAA includes 7:1 contrast for normal text.

The four WCAG principles are POUR: Perceivable, Operable, Understandable and Robust.
