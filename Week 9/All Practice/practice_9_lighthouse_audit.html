Practice 9 - Lighthouse Audit

Run an automated accessibility audit and understand its limitations.

Quick review

  1. Open the page in Chrome and select Inspect (F12).
  2. Open the Lighthouse tab.
  3. Select Accessibility.
  4. Click Analyze page load.
  5. Review the score and expand failed checks.

Score guide in the material: 90–100 excellent, 50–89 needs improvement, 0–49 significant issues.

Lighthouse catches only some accessibility issues. A high score does not replace keyboard testing and real screen-reader testing.
