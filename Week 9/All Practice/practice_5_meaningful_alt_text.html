Practice 5 - Meaningful Alt Text

Write text alternatives based on the purpose of each image.

Quick review

  • Informational image: describe the information it conveys.
  • Decorative image: use alt="".
  • Linked image: describe the link destination.
  • Complex chart: provide a short alt and a longer description nearby.
