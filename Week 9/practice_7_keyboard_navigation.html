Practice 7 - Keyboard Navigation

Make every interactive part of a website usable without a mouse.

Quick review

  • Tab: move forward through focusable controls.
  • Shift + Tab: move backward.
  • Enter / Space: activate links and buttons as appropriate.
  • Arrow keys: move within components such as radio groups and menus.

Use :focus-visible to show a clear keyboard focus indicator. Do not remove outlines without providing an alternative.
